Notes on the model

Stinger is FTC team 21592 Fast But Not Furious (FBNF)'s robot. This is a mock-up rebuilt from match video, not the team's CAD. The layout is right (front cast-silicone intake on a swing arm, straight linear transfer, turret, underhand shooter on two goBILDA Hogback 72 mm wheels, belt-driven from a motor mounted beside them). The shooter is measured off the side-view footage using the 72 mm wheel as the scale; other sizes are estimates.

Hive is the official BIOBUZZ HIVE: pivot 43.95 in up, arm tilted 30°, upward cell opening 53.5–65.6 in above the tiles. Hive distance = front of the robot to the hive pivot.

Turret. Angle to hive puts the hive off to the side. The chassis never turns; the turret ring gear swings the shooter to face it.

Robot numbers. Wheel size, grip, motor speed, belt ratio, ball mass and topspin are estimates. Change them in the Math tab once you've measured the real robot; the link in the address bar keeps your numbers.

Dashed line = the next shot, or the ball in the air. Auto speed picks the exit speed that drops the ball through the middle of the opening. Air drag is on (holed balls are light); Cd = 0.5 is an estimate.

Slow motion. The intake, transfer and wheel are slowed right down so you can follow the ball; the flight plays at 0.45× real time.
